# Privacy policy: Viet Bake Shop

*Draft. Last updated: 10/02/2026. Contact: myvientrannguyen@gmail.com.*

Viet Bake Shop is a single-player game. It is designed to collect nothing about you.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. It doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else.

## What stays on your device

Your bakery (the save game, including the bakery name and the first name or nickname you may type) and your settings (sound, text size, and so on) are stored on your device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to share them:

- **Save codes:** if you copy a save code, it contains only your game data. Where you paste it is up to you.
- **Email restore link** (web version only): this opens *your own* email app with a link containing your save code. We never see the email or your address.

Uninstalling the app (or clearing your browser's site data) deletes your saves.

## Permissions

The mobile app doesn't request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. (Android lists a standard internet permission that the app framework includes; the game itself works offline and makes no network requests.)

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if optional cloud saves are added), the new version will be published here before the change ships, and the game will ask before sending anything anywhere.
