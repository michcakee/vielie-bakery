Privacy Policy

Viet Bake Shop Simulator · last updated 3 October 2026

The short version

Viet Bake Shop Simulator is a single-player game. It collects no personal information. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. It never sends anything to us.

In-app purchases (iPhone and iPad app only). Diamond packs are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your name, Apple ID, email or payment details. Apple tells the app only which pack was bought and a transaction number, and that stays on your device. Your device contacts the App Store only when you open the diamond shop or buy something.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved game (bakery name, progress, settings) is stored in the app's local storage on your device: the browser's storage on the web, the app's private storage on a phone.
  • Save codes: if you copy a save code, it contains only your game data. Where you paste it is up to you.
  • "Email me a restore link" (web version): this opens your own email app with a link containing your save code. The address you type is used once to open that email and is not stored by the game. We never see the email or the address.
  • The bakery name and the first name or nickname you type stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. You can leave the name blank.
  • Diamonds and the Dream team you unlock, and the transaction numbers of App Store purchases (so a purchase is never counted twice), are stored on your device and shared by every bakery you play on it.

Third parties

No data is shared with or sold to anyone. Purchases in the iOS app are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The web version is served from GitHub Pages, whose servers may keep ordinary web-server logs (such as your IP address) when the page loads; the game itself sends nothing after that.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. In the game, open Settings → Delete all my data to erase every save, backup and setting, including your diamonds and Dream team (purchased diamonds can't be restored afterwards). Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser's site data, does the same.

Children's privacy

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It contains no ads, no chat and no way to contact other players. The iPhone and iPad app offers optional diamond packs; Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time let a parent approve or turn off purchases.

Permissions

The mobile app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if optional cloud saves were added), the new version will be published here before the change ships, and the game will ask before sending anything anywhere.
