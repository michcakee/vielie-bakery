# Accessibility

We want every kid to be able to run Bà's bakery. This page says what the game supports today, what doesn't work well yet, and how to tell us about a barrier.

## Our goal

We aim to meet [WCAG 2.1 level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) wherever a fast-paced game allows, and to offer a slower, calmer way to play wherever it doesn't.

## What's supported

**Settings (the gear in the top bar):**
- **Relaxed pace** is on by default, with slower days and more patient customers. You can **Pause** any time during service.
- **Text size** controls.
- **Reduce motion** turns off moving decorations. The game also follows your device's reduced-motion setting.
- **Plain English everywhere.** Only Bà speaks Vietnamese (always with English underneath), and food keeps its Vietnamese name with an English description.
- **Sound effects** and **music** can each be turned off, and nothing in the game depends on hearing a sound.
- **Break reminder** is an optional gentle note after 30 minutes.

**While playing:**
- **Help on demand.** The **?** button shows what to do right now, and **Show me** points at the right button.
- **Keyboard.** Every control is a real button or input. In service, number keys pick a customer and Space pauses. In the oven game, Space or Enter takes the tray out. There's a "Skip to controls" link.
- **Screen readers.** Buttons, meters and the star goal have labels, and order results are announced.
- **Phones.** Buttons are at least 44 pixels tall on touch screens, and nothing needs a two-finger gesture or a hover.
- **No timing traps.** You can skip the oven game with **Quick bake**. During service you can tap **Bà, help!** (and **I'll serve** to take back over) or **Skip to closing**. For a whole day you can let Bà or the team run it.

## Supported environments

Recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on Windows, macOS, ChromeOS, Android and iOS. The game also runs as an installable web app and as Android and iOS apps.

## Known limitations

- The bakery scene is pixel art drawn as images. Customers and what they want are also listed as text below it, but the scene itself isn't described in detail.
- Serving customers is timed, even at the relaxed pace. Players who need more time can pause, hand the counter to Bà, or let the team run the day.
- The oven mini-game asks you to tap at the right moment. Quick bake skips it, at a fair quality.
- Some colour meanings, like the demand meter and patience faces, also have words or icons, but we haven't yet tested every screen with colour-blindness simulators.
- Vietnamese text depends on your device's fonts.

## Report a barrier

If something stops you or your child from playing, please [open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) and pick **Bug report**. Start the title with "Accessibility:". Tell us your device, browser, any assistive technology you use, and what got in the way. We read every one, and accessibility fixes come first.
