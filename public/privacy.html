The short version

Viet Bake Shop is a single-player game. It collects no personal information. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. It makes no network requests to us or to anyone else while you play.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved game (bakery name, progress, settings) is stored in the app's local storage on your device: the browser's storage on the web, the app's private storage on a phone.
  • Save codes: if you copy a save code, it contains only your game data. Where you paste it is up to you.
  • "Email me a restore link" (web version): this opens your own email app with a link containing your save code. The address you type is used once to open that email and is not stored by the game. We never see the email or the address.
  • The bakery name you type stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Third parties

None. No data is shared with or sold to anyone. The web version is served from GitHub Pages, whose servers may keep ordinary web-server logs (such as your IP address) when the page loads; the game itself sends nothing after that.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. In the game, open Settings → Delete all my data to erase every save, backup and setting. Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser's site data, does the same.

Children's privacy

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It contains no ads, no purchases, no chat and no way to contact other players.

Permissions

The mobile app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if optional cloud saves were added), the new version will be published here before the change ships, and the game will ask before sending anything anywhere.
